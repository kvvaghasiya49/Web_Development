About StudentHub

Why this portal exists, and what it's built to solve.

The problem

Most college students juggle three or four separate places just to stay on top of academics: a physical notice board for announcements, a fee receipt book, an attendance register the class teacher keeps, and word of mouth for everything else. StudentHub brings those into a single portal so a student can check where they stand without visiting four different offices.

Mission

Our mission is to make everyday college tasks easier for students by bringing attendance, fees, and announcements together on one simple, dependable platform.

Built with

This project was built as part of the Web Development Frameworks course, one practical at a time.

HTML5 & CSS3

Semantic markup and a shared stylesheet for every page, styled with Flexbox and a consistent spacing system.

JavaScript

Handles form validation, dynamic content from JSON, and interactive UI elements like the FAQ toggle.

PHP & MySQL

Powers registration, login sessions, and the attendance, fees, and announcements data behind the scenes.

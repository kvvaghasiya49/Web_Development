About StudentHub

StudentHub is a simple web portal built for college students to manage their day-to-day academic needs in one place.

It allows students to check their attendance, view fee details, read college announcements, and share feedback without running around different offices.

This project is built as a part of the Web Development Frameworks course.

Mission / vision statement

Our mission is to make everyday college tasks easier for students by bringing attendance, fees, and announcements together on one simple platform.
